# Overstreet

Scheduling app for **Overstreet Cleaning Solutions** (Aida Overstreet).

Built from her intake: paper calendar → day / week / month board, job type (regular vs deep vs extras), house notes, repeating jobs, payment tracking, maps, mileage, clock in/out, and tap-to-text reminders.

## Open it

- App: `overstreet/index.html` (Android: Add to Home screen)
- Public booking request (no client accounts): `overstreet/book.html?to=HERNUMBER`

Preview on this PR after Railway deploys: `/overstreet/`

## In this version

Must-haves from Aida:

- Calendar by day, week, and month, color-coded by job type
- House file: address, sq ft, beds/baths/stories, pets, access, codes, surfaces, do-not-touch
- Regular / deep / Airbnb / post-construction / organization
- Extras: baseboards, blinds, windows, appliances, etc. (not in a standard clean)
- Repeating weekly, every 2 weeks, or monthly
- Track what they owe; collect cash / Zelle / Cash App / check at the job (minimum $200)
- Directions in Google Maps
- Miles on each job + month total
- Clock in / clock out
- Night-before list to herself, client reminder, booking confirm, thank-you (opens Messages)
- Waitlist
- Optional PIN
- Add to Google Calendar / download .ics

Later, on purpose: client logins, tap-through checklists, supplies, full helper schedules, in-app card payments, automatic SMS without tapping (needs a texting service).
