## Rooms and extras

**Job types you offer**
Regular / maintenance clean, Deep clean, Airbnb / short-term rental turnover, Post-construction, Organization help, Other (describe below)

**What is included in a standard clean vs a deep clean?**
The standard clean doesn't include baseboards, blinds, windows, appliances inside.

**Rooms**
- Kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living, dining, hallways/stairs/entry, laundry room, office: always included
- Dishes, change linens / make beds, take out trash: always included
- Basement, patio/deck/porch: add-on
- Garage: do not offer
- Interior/exterior windows, blinds/shutters, baseboards, ceiling fans: add-on
- Inside fridge, oven, microwave, cabinets/drawers: add-on
- Wash / fold laundry: add-on
- Walls / spot cleaning: depends on the job
