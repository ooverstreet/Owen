# Cleaning app intake

A planner for a house-cleaning scheduling app. Fill it out, send the answers back, then we build.

## For her (the cleaner)

Two ways to fill this out:

1. **On a phone or computer** — open `index.html` (the form). It saves as you type on that device. At the end, tap **Copy answers** or **Download** and send the file.
2. **On paper** — print `INTAKE.md` or use **Print a paper copy** in the form. Handwritten answers are fine.

Skip anything. Partial answers are still useful. Stories about real jobs help more than perfect checklists.

Nothing is uploaded. Answers stay on the device until they are copied or downloaded.

## For you (what else we need)

Her answers are the main ingredient. These extras make the first version much better if they exist — none are required to start:

**Pictures and artifacts**

- Photo of her current calendar (phone, paper, or app)
- Current checklist or “what’s included” list
- Price list or typical quote she has sent
- Logo, van wrap, shirts, or colors she already uses
- Screenshot of how a client books her today (a text thread is perfect)

**Decisions you can make together after she fills this out**

- Phone only vs also a computer at home
- Private app for her vs a public booking link for clients
- Whether money is tracked in the app or still Venmo / cash
- Whether texts should be automatic (that needs a phone number and a small messaging budget) or she keeps texting herself
- Whether Google / Apple Calendar should stay in the loop

**What I do not need yet**

- A company LLC, domain, or professional branding — nice later, not blocking
- Payment-processor accounts unless she wants clients to pay in the app
- A full employee roster unless she already has helpers

When the filled form comes back, the next step is a first version around her must-haves (usually: calendar, house notes, recurring jobs).
